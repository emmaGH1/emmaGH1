## twists

a constraint that forces a different interaction.

- no screen: voice or audio only
- works over whatsapp only
- the user can't read
- two people use it at the same time
- it must take under 10 seconds
- it works offline and syncs later
- it runs without the user opening it
- the output is a physical thing (receipt, sticker, printout)
- the user pays for it with something other than money
- it gets better the more people in the same street use it
- the expert keeps their job and gets paid more
- it only matters once a year
- it's used by the person who causes the problem, not the victim
- it has to be trusted by people who distrust each other
