# the lane

> **proposed; confirm or change it.** serial winners keep building variations on one theme, so every event starts from working parts and past judgement (`cases.md`). this is ours.

## the lane

**ai agents that move everyday people's money safely, with receipts.**

- **why it fits you:** nigerian cash habits, credit and contributions, crypto communities. the sponsors you enter most (agent payments, stablecoin wallets, chains, agent tooling) want exactly this.
- **why it wins:** the product makes its own proof. a refused move, a verified transaction and a receipt are evidence the moment they happen.
- **the shape of every entry:** a real person with money at stake + the sponsor's product doing the money step + the core (`templates/core/`) doing the guard, evidence and receipts + telegram or whatsapp as the way in.

## outside the lane

still fine when an event clearly fits something else (roux was a restaurant picker and won). then use the core where it helps and pick the idea on the normal tests.
