# step 4: design (timeboxed)

> budget: about 10% of the clock in total. design serves the judge path; it never replaces a working product.

## anti-slop (don't, unless the product truly needs it)

- gradient, glow, blob, aurora or glassmorphism backgrounds; gradient text
- "live / online / agent active" dots and pills that don't reflect a real state
- fake terminals, logs, activity feeds, dashboard metrics, testimonials, logos
- endless identical rounded cards; hero → three feature cards → faq templates
- sparkles, bot, brain or zap icons as shorthand for ai; emoji as icons
- a chat ui when chat isn't the product
- default template typography chosen without a reason

## direction in 30 minutes

- 3 concrete words ("quiet editorial", "dense instrument", "warm consumer"), not "clean" or "modern"
- one visual motif that comes from what the product does
- one or two type families, chosen for this product
- if the human is around: render the first screen in 3 directions as screenshots and let them pick (a minute for them). if not: pick the one that fits the 3 words.
- write the locked direction in `AGENTS.md` so every session uses it

## landing page = the poster

- a product-specific promise, one primary action ("try it, no wallet needed"), real product proof on the first screen
- the product is one click away
- passes the x screenshot test: the first screen looks intentional with no explanation
- same type, palette and motif as the app

## the product screen

- readable at phone width
- the journey's result is the biggest thing on the screen
- success is obvious; loading shows real progress
- nothing the judge needs is hover-only or hidden in a menu

## logo (30 minutes max)

- a wordmark with one custom detail taken from the motif, hand-written svg
- check it at 16px, on light and dark
- export `logo.svg`, favicon, `og.png` (1200×630: logo plus the one-line promise)
- no sparkles, brains, robot heads, hexagons, globes, letters in gradient squares, or more than two colours
